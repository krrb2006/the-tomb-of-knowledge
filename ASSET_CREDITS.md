source, author, and license were not recorded in the repository history. Treat
their redistribution status as **unverified**. Do not describe them as royalty-
free or ship a public release until the provenance is confirmed and documented.

modifications, and any required attribution. If the details cannot be verified,
replace the file with an asset whose license is clear.
